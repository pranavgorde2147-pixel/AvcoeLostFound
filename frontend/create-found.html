🛡️ How Verification Works

When someone claims this item, they will be asked your verification question. Only the true owner will know the answer.

Good examples:

  • "What color is the inside lining of the bag?"
  • "What sticker is on the back of the laptop?"
  • "What is written on the first page of the notebook?"
  • "How many compartments does the wallet have?"

Tips:

  • Be specific — the question should have a clear, verifiable answer
  • Don't include the answer in the question itself
  • Avoid questions that could be guessed easily
